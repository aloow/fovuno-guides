# Fovuno AI Product Ad Generator: From Product Photo to Static Ad

English · [中文](#中文商品广告生成器使用说明)

Have a product photo but need an advertising image with a different setting or layout? Fovuno’s AI Product Ad Generator lets you upload the product, choose a visual direction, and describe the image you want to make.

This guide is maintained by Fovuno and was prepared with AI assistance. The prompt below is an illustrative starting point, not a tested result.

## Choose how to start

- **Built-in template:** start with one of the available ad references.
- **Your own reference:** upload an image to guide the composition and visual style. Use a reference you have permission to use.
- **Free creation:** describe a scene without choosing a template. This means creating without a reference, not generating at no cost.

Your product photo supplies the product details. A reference supplies the visual direction; check that its product, logo, or promotional text has not carried into your result.

## Make your first image

1. Open [Fovuno’s product ad generator](https://fovuno.com/ai-product-ad-generator).
2. Upload one clear JPG or PNG product photo, up to 10 MB.
3. Choose a template, upload a reference, or select **Free creation**.
4. Describe the background, lighting, placement, and any text you want. Choose the aspect ratio and resolution for your intended placement.
5. Sign in, check the displayed credit quote, and generate with sufficient credits. Review the result before downloading the PNG.

For a simple scene without a reference, adapt this prompt:

> Create a static ad image using my uploaded product photo. Preserve the product’s shape, color, material, logo, and packaging. Place it on a warm cream surface with soft window light. Keep the background simple and leave empty space above the product for a headline I will add later. Do not add extra products, text, prices, or discount badges.

## Check before using it

Compare the output with your original photo: inspect the silhouette, proportions, label, logo, material, and color. A prompt can request preservation, but generation can still alter these details. Also check the crop at the size where the ad will appear.

If you need exact promotional wording, leaving room to add it in an image editor can make correction easier. This workflow produces a static image; you handle ad placement and performance testing separately.

## 中文：商品广告生成器使用说明

有商品照片，但想换一个适合广告的场景或构图？Fovuno 商品广告生成器可以让你上传商品图、选择视觉方向，再描述希望生成的图片。

本文由 Fovuno 维护，使用 AI 辅助编写。下方提示词是示例起点，尚不是实测案例。

### 选择起点

- **内置模板：**从页面提供的广告参考中选择。
- **自定义参考：**上传你有权使用的图片，作为构图和风格参考。
- **自由创作：**不选择模板，直接描述场景。这里的“自由”指无需参考图，不代表免费生成。

商品图提供商品细节，参考图提供视觉方向。生成后要检查，参考图中的商品、标志和促销文字是否被错误带入。

### 完成第一张图片

1. 打开 [Fovuno 商品广告生成器](https://fovuno.com/zh/ai-product-ad-generator)。
2. 上传一张清晰的 JPG 或 PNG 商品图，文件不超过 10 MB。
3. 选择内置模板、上传自定义参考，或使用自由创作。
4. 描述背景、光线、商品位置和所需文字，再选择适合投放位置的比例与分辨率。
5. 登录并查看页面显示的积分报价，积分充足时提交生成。检查结果后下载 PNG。

如果使用自由创作，可以从这段提示词开始调整：

> 使用我上传的商品照片制作一张静态广告图。保留商品的形状、颜色、材质、标志和包装。将商品放在暖奶油色台面上，使用柔和的窗边光。背景保持简洁，在商品上方留出空白，方便我稍后添加标题。不要添加其他商品、文字、价格或折扣标签。

### 使用前检查

对照原图检查轮廓、比例、标签、标志、材质和颜色。提示词可以要求保留细节，但生成仍可能改变这些内容。还要按实际展示尺寸检查裁切。

如果广告文案必须准确，可以先留白，再用图片编辑器排字，便于修改。这里生成的是静态图片；广告投放和效果测试需要另行完成。
